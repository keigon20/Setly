Frequently asked questions

How do I add a show to my journal?

On the Journal tab, tap the + button. Search for the event, or enter the details yourself, then save it.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to reset it. If you signed up with Google or Apple, sign in with that option instead.

How do I report a bug?

In the app, go to Profile, tap the Settings icon, then tap Report a Bug. You can also email us.

How do I report or block someone?

To report or block someone from a post, tap the ··· button on it. To report a comment or reply, press and hold it. You can see and unblock people you've blocked on your friends screen.

Can I keep a show private?

Yes. When you add or edit a show, turn on Hide from friends. Friends won't see it in their feed, but it stays in your journal.

How do I change my notifications?

Go to Profile, tap the Settings icon, then tap Notification Settings. You can turn each type on or off, including reminders for upcoming shows.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, tap the Settings icon, then tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data. If you can't sign in, see Delete Your Account.
